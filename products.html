Hardware, Software and Lab-Ready Solution Kits

Product Range

Trainer Kits & Lab Equipment Across Engineering Domains

From core electrical fundamentals to emerging Industry 4.0 concepts, our product lines are grouped around the same domains our engineering team works in every day.

Embedded & Microcontroller Kits

Trainer boards spanning popular microcontroller and microprocessor families, suited for coursework and rapid firmware prototyping.

IoT & Wireless Connectivity Kits

Gateway hardware and wireless sensor node kits for assembling complete, demonstrable IoT setups.

Automation Trainer Panels

Panels built around PLC and SCADA platforms that reproduce real industrial control logic for classroom and R&D use.

Power Electronics & Drive Trainers

Rectifier, inverter and motor-drive modules for studying power conversion and control circuits hands-on.

Control Systems Trainer Kits

Bench-top platforms for exploring open-loop and closed-loop control across servo and process examples.

Process & Instrumentation Trainers

Level, flow, pressure and temperature trainer modules that build practical instrumentation skills.

Power Systems & Smart Grid Equipment

Network analyzers and renewable-integration trainers for studying distribution networks and smart grid behavior.

Communication & DSP Trainers

Modulation, demodulation and digital signal processing kits covering analog and digital communication basics.

RF & Microwave Lab Kits

Signal source and detector modules built for hands-on RF and microwave measurement experiments.

VLSI & FPGA Development Kits

FPGA and VLSI trainer boards for digital logic design, simulation and hardware description language practice.

Robotics & Mechatronics Kits

Robotic-arm and mechatronics platforms that combine mechanical, electrical and control concepts in one build.

EV & Battery Lab Kits

Electric vehicle subsystem and battery management demonstration kits for EV-focused laboratories.

Data Acquisition Systems

Configurable DAQ hardware paired with software dashboards for capturing and visualizing real-world measurements.

Industry 4.0 & Digital Twin Kits

Connected-manufacturing demonstration setups that pair physical hardware with a digital twin model.

Electrical Trainer Boards

Panels and boards for teaching core electrical and electronic system concepts through practical circuits.
